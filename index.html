Google Drive Integration & Sync

How MS Sister safely handles your Google Drive data with complete transparency.

MS Sister utilizes the official Google Drive API to provide a zero-knowledge cloud backup solution. We believe intimate health data should remain strictly between you and your personal devices. Here is how our sync process works:

1

On-Device Encryption

Before any backup leaves your phone, MS Sister packages your cycle records and logs into a single payload encrypted with hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption.

2

Direct Drive Upload

The app connects directly from your phone to Google Drive servers via HTTPS. The encrypted payload is saved into your personal, restricted Google Drive App Data folder.

3

Zero Intermediate Server

MS Sister operates without any backend database or intermediate app server. Neither the developer (Mukesh) nor any third party ever receives, transmits, or sees your data.

4

Full User Control

You can enable, disable, or delete your Google Drive backup file at any time directly through Google Drive settings or by logging out within the app.

Official Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
MS Sister's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google Drive data for advertising, analytics, machine learning training, or third-party sharing.
