Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Package: com.mukesh.mssister

Summary: MS Sister is built on a Zero-Server, Zero-Telemetry Architecture. Your intimate menstrual cycle data, symptom logs, and medical notes are stored exclusively on your device using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption. When you enable cloud backup, your data is synced directly to your personal Google Drive storage. We do not collect, sell, or view your health records.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MS Sister ("we", "us", "our", or "the App"), developed by Mukesh (Package Name: com.mukesh.mssister), handles your information when you use our mobile application. Your privacy is fundamental to our mission. We believe intimate health records belong solely to you.

2. Data Collection & Usage

MS Sister is designed to operate locally on your Android device.

  • Personal Health Data: Your cycle start dates, period durations, symptom logs, flow intensity levels, pain scores, pain locations, moods, physical symptoms, and doctor notes are stored locally on your device in EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • No Third-Party Analytics / Telemetry: We do not track your app activity, sell data to advertisers, or utilize third-party telemetry tools.
  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in with your Google account, we retrieve your email address solely to establish identity for Google Drive authorization and display your profile. You can mask your email display on screen at any time within the Settings tab.

3. Google Drive Integration & Google OAuth Scopes

MS Sister provides an optional feature allowing users to back up and restore their health data across devices using their personal Google Drive storage.

Scope Requested

The app requests access to the Google Drive API using restricted access scopes (such as https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata or drive.file).

How Drive Data is Synced

  1. Before syncing, MS Sister packages your cycle and symptom records into a JSON payload.
  2. The payload is encrypted on your phone using Android KeyStore AES-256-GCM hardware encryption.
  3. The encrypted payload is uploaded directly via secure HTTPS to your personal, private Google Drive storage space.
  4. When restoring data on a new device, the encrypted file is fetched directly from your Google Drive and decrypted locally.
Official Google Limited Use Disclosure:
MS Sister's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:
  • Google Drive data is never transmitted to MS Sister servers (we have no external backend database).
  • Google Drive data is never used for serving advertisements, marketing, or behavioral tracking.
  • Google Drive data is never shared with third parties or used for artificial intelligence/machine learning model training.

4. Security & Encryption

We employ industry-leading security practices to safeguard your data:

  • Hardware-Backed AES-256-GCM Encryption: Master keys are generated and stored securely inside the Android KeyStore System.
  • EncryptedSharedPreferences: Local settings and cycle metrics are encrypted before being written to storage.
  • Biometric & PIN Lock: You can protect app access using fingerprint, face recognition, or custom PIN locking.
  • Stealth Launcher Icons: You can switch between 3 discreet launcher icons on your home screen to conceal the app's identity.

5. Doctor PDF Report Generation

The PDF Doctor Report feature generates an A4 PDF document locally on your device cache directory. This file remains on your phone until you manually export, print, or share it with your gynecologist or healthcare provider.

6. User Data Control & Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control over your data:

  • Local Data Deletion: Clearing app data or uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally stored health records.
  • Google Drive Deletion: You can delete your encrypted backup file directly from your Google Drive account under "Manage Apps" settings at any time.
  • Revoking OAuth Access: You can revoke MS Sister's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Security Page.

7. Children's Privacy

MS Sister is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
